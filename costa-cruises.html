CRUISE COMPANIES/ COSTA CRUISES
COSTA CRUISE
The Italian style of Costa, to experience the world from a different perspective, that of the sea

On Costa ships you can choose from plenty of experiences offered to fulfil a variety of tastes, yours and those of the people travelling with you. Everything is there for you to appreciate from a truly unique perspective: that of the ship and the sea. All in the true Italian style that has always distinguished Costa and with the great passion of our on-board staff who will make you feel at home for the entire holiday.


Costa Cruises cabins. Find your oasis of comfort

Every Costa cabin features: adjustable air conditioning with thermostat; interactive flat-screen satellite TV; hair dryer; safe; a steward, always on duty; minibar and 24-hour room service (charges apply); Wi-Fi (charges apply); soap and shower gel; linen (bed sheets and towels) changed regularly. Each type of cabin has different features: Inside, Sea View and Sea View with Private Balcony. Cabins are available that are larger or in a better position on the decks of the ship, depending on the rate selected.


CostaTours! (Shore Excursions) all over the world

With Costa every stopover is a holiday within a holiday!

There are so many CostaTours!, you are bound to find the perfect one for you. Go to MyCosta to choose amongst the broad offering of attractions or tours that you really would like to experience during your cruise. There are all types of excursions available: adventurous and sporty, shopping and traditional sightseeing tours: You can also choose from a range of relaxing trips discovering the local nature and culture, perfect for both our older and younger guests, to more energetic and sports-oriented outings for people who like to stay active during their holiday.


An action filled day

You will never be bored on a Costa holiday!
Every day there is something to do for everyone! Whether you are a sports enthusiast, lover of relaxation, adventure seeker, yound or old – there are so many activities to choose from and spend your holiday days to the fullest. There are multi-sport pitches on board where you can play all kinds of team games, from 5-a-side football to volleyball and basketball. And don’t forget the pleasures of shopping. Our onboard shops are open when the ship is at sea.


Restaurants

All the restaurants serve specialities made with top-quality ingredients and prepared with the love of food that Italians are famous for.
